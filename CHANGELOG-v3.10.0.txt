Thanksgiving v3.10.0 — Hero Experience Studio

CUSTOMER HERO
- The existing landing hero is now configurable from Admin.
- Six hero models:
  1. Drink / Order Counter
  2. Drink / Vinyl Bar
  3. Food / Cafe Plate
  4. Food / Dessert Session
  5. Food + Drink / Perfect Pairing
  6. Food + Drink / Editorial Duo
- Hero colors automatically follow Primary / Background / Accent from Brand Kit.
- Hero logo uses the current Admin brand logo.
- Food mode changes regular headline to "Makananmu punya soundtrack."
- Food + Drink mode changes regular headline to "Food & drink-mu punya soundtrack."
- Product chip icon follows Drink / Food / Combo.
- Phone player, order card, and Meong bubble can each be enabled/disabled.
- Optional custom headline and custom Meong line.
- Fixed model or random rotation across enabled models.

ADMIN
- New "Hero Experience Studio" inside Brand & Vibe.
- Choose Drink / Food / Food+Drink.
- Choose main model.
- Enable/disable models in Model Library.
- Tap a model preview to make it the main model.
- Fixed or Random display mode.
- Optional custom headline and Meong copy.
- Toggle phone player / order card / Meong.

RESPONSIVE
- Dedicated mobile scaling down to 335px.
- Tablet/desktop landing becomes a two-column hero/content composition.
- Hero objects reposition per breakpoint to avoid collision.

SAFETY
- Based directly on v3.9.9.1 cassette color hotfix.
- Existing Welcome, Occasion, QR, Music Cut Studio, Video Final Editor, share pipeline,
  gacha, audio, menu and cassette intro are retained.
- firebase-config.js is not included or modified.
- database.rules.json is unchanged.
