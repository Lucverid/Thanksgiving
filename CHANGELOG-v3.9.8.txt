- Clip durations: 5s / 7s / 10s (disabled automatically if the audio is shorter).
- Preview the selected clip before recording.
- "Dari awal" resets the cut.
- "Cari bagian seru" finds a higher-energy waveform section locally.
- Motion Studio shows the chosen cut and lets the user edit it again.
- Video recording seeks the selected song to that exact timestamp and uses the chosen clip duration.
- After recording, music pauses and returns to the selected cut start.
