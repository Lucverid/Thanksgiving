Thanksgiving v3.9.9 — QR Flow + Video Final Editor + Duration Sync

QR ADMIN
- General Experience is now the default QR type.
- General QR does not ask Admin to choose an occasion; customer chooses from enabled occasions.
- Dedicated Occasion remains optional for Birthday/Wedding/etc.
- List QR now has Download QR on each row and in the saved preview.
- List QR edit can switch General/Dedicated.

VIDEO
- Duration choices: 5 / 10 / 30 seconds.

- Camera take is captured raw first.
- A Final Video Editor appears after recording, before download/share.
- User can change the motion design and move logo, brand name, and caption without retaking.
- Final render bakes the edited template + selected song cut.

SHARE DURATION HOTFIX
- WebM → MP4 conversion regenerates PTS from a fixed 30fps frame count.
- MP4 output is forced to the selected duration, fixing 10s video becoming ~3–4s in share.
